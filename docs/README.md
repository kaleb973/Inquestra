## Scientific Scope & Domains

Inquestra is designed for cross-disciplinary research teams across:
- **Molecular Biology & Genomics:** Oligonucleotide property calculation ($T_m$, GC%), sequence manipulation, and plasmid workflows.
- **Wet-Lab Chemistry:** Live PubChem compound search, 2D molecular rendering from SMILES, and buffer preparation logs.
- **Materials Science & Mechanical Testing:** Sensor data acquisition, stress/strain charts, and equipment calibration records.
- **Hardware Telemetry & Instrumentation:** Real-time Web Serial streaming from microcontrollers, scales, and bench instruments.
