# Academic Bench Positioning

Inquestra will position as a lightweight, zero-friction research notebook for academic researchers (students, postdocs, and research assistants) rather than an enterprise compliance LIMS.

## Status

accepted

## Context

Enterprise biotech companies require GxP validation, vendor security audits, and formal compliance certifications that cannot be credibly serviced by an independent solo developer. Academic researchers face daily documentation friction, lack grant budget for commercial enterprise tools, and seek fast, reliable, open tools that do not lock their data.

## Decision

We will focus product development, terminology, and messaging on academic bench researchers. Marketing will emphasize speed, data ownership (open export), protocol execution, and zero signup friction. We explicitly defer enterprise compliance selling (21 CFR Part 11 validation, SOC-2 guarantees) until institutional demand justifies it.

## Consequences

- Strips out enterprise sales bloat from the marketing and onboarding experience.
- Aligns product priorities with daily bench execution rather than administrative compliance overhead.
- Enables viral, bottom-up adoption through academic labs and student research teams.
