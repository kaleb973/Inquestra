# iGEM and Student Wet-Lab Distribution Wedge

Inquestra will target student research teams, specifically iGEM (International Genetically Engineered Machine) competition teams and academic student wet-labs, as the initial distribution channel to achieve early user adoption.

## Status

accepted

## Context

Cold outreach to enterprise labs and senior Principal Investigators suffers from bureaucratic procurement delays and existing vendor lock-in. Student research teams, such as iGEM teams, are mandated to maintain open lab notebooks, possess zero software budget, and are early adopters of modern, open tooling.

## Decision

We will focus our direct outreach and initial template offerings on student synthetic biology and wet-lab teams. The value proposition will emphasize zero-friction access, instant collaboration, open data export, and zero cost.

## Consequences

- Landing page and starter templates should highlight standard wet-lab protocols (e.g., PCR, gel electrophoresis, restriction digests).
- Direct outreach can be conducted peer-to-peer without corporate sales friction.
- Establishes a pipeline of future graduate students and industry researchers who carry familiarity with Inquestra into their future labs.
